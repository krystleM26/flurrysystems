Your Value Proposition

Practical ImplementationYou do not just consult — you actually build and deploy AI solutions
Ongoing OptimizationMonthly reviews and adjustments ensure clients get continuous value
Team TrainingYou ensure adoption through hands-on training and documentation

Core Service Components

1. Discovery & Workflow Audit (Week 1)

Interview key team members, map current workflows, identify AI opportunities with highest ROI potential.

Deliverable: Workflow analysis document with 3–5 prioritized AI implementation opportunities

2. AI Solution Design (Week 2)

Design custom AI agents and integrations, select appropriate tools and platforms, create implementation roadmap.

Deliverable: Technical implementation plan with tool selections and timeline

3. Implementation (Weeks 3–4)

Build and configure AI agents, integrate with existing tools, test workflows thoroughly, create documentation.

Deliverable: Deployed AI solutions with user documentation

4. Training & Handoff (Week 5)

Conduct hands-on training sessions, provide SOPs and guides, establish success metrics.

Deliverable: Trained team with operational AI workflows

5. Ongoing Support (Monthly)

Monthly optimization reviews, troubleshooting support, updates for new AI capabilities, performance reporting.

Deliverable: Monthly report with metrics, improvements, and recommendations
Pro Tip: Start with one workflow type and perfect it. Then replicate this proven solution across multiple clients before expanding to other workflow types.

Client Acquisition Strategy

1. LinkedIn Outreach (Weeks 1–2)

Identify 50–100 decision-makers in your target industries. Share valuable content and reach out with personalized messages.

Template: Hi [Name], I noticed [Company] is in [industry]. I help businesses save 10–15 hours per week by automating [specific workflow]. Open to a 15-minute chat?
2. Free Workflow Audit (Weeks 2–4)

Offer 5 free 30-minute workflow audits. Identify 2–3 AI opportunities. Convert 40–60% to paid clients.

3. Industry-Specific Content

Create LinkedIn posts and case studies showing specific AI implementations in your target industries.

4. Referral Network

Partner with complementary service providers who can refer clients. Offer 10–15% referral fees.
